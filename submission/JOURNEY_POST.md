# Our SupeRR Selector journey

**The Pressure Pair · Harsh Sinha and Mosin Shaikh**

Six balls left. Ten to win. Now picture the same over with two to win. How much does the next ball matter in each chase?

That question shaped our Round 1 entry. We used 763 IPL and T20I matches to estimate the stakes before each delivery and to compare each player with what the situation called for and with their own usual level.

Dinesh Karthik and Ravindra Jadeja lead our lists from the names in the Unstop form. Bowling was the harder call. A balanced score improved the broader bowling results in three later seasons, but the small Unstop pool gave us too little separation to change the order. That was our reminder to show the sample behind every score.

Thanks to @rajasthanroyals and @unstop.world for the challenge. Halla Bol! #SuperSelector
