# My SupeRR Selector journey

**Prepared by Harsh Sinha**

Six balls left. Ten to win. Now imagine the same over with two to win. How much does the next ball really matter in each chase?

That question shaped my Round 1 entry for SupeRR Selector Season 2. I used 763 IPL and T20I matches to estimate the stakes before each delivery, then compared what each player did with what the match situation called for and with their own usual level.

My favourite finding was Vaibhav Sooryavanshi. His strike rate dipped under pressure, from 222 to 206, but his context-adjusted advantage improved. Expected scoring and dismissals tell a fuller story than the strike rate alone.

Ashutosh Sharma and Joshua Little lead my batting and bowling lists. The tight calls near tenth place were the most interesting part of the exercise. The submission needs an exact order, and the evidence still leaves room for debate.
