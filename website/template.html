04

Test the assumptions

Combine three pressure cutoffs and three wicket values. Rank the 20th percentile of 2,000 whole-match resamples.

The exact formulas and eligibility rules +
Pressure = C × L × U
C = 4p(1−p)
L = 0.30 max(p6−p0, 0) + 0.055 max(p0−pw, 0)
U = 1 + balls used / 120

The reference cutoff is the 75th percentile across all retained first-innings events, computed separately from the 75th percentile across all chase events. The robust rating tests the top 30%, 25% and 20%, with wickets valued at 8, 12 and 16 run-equivalent units.

Two chases with six balls left. With illustrative win probabilities, a chase needing ten runs scores 0.3453 and one needing two runs scores 0.0023. Only the first crosses the measured chase cutoff of 0.0394. Slide 3 of the PDF shows the pressure calculation.

A = pressure residual sum / (pressure opportunities + 120)
B = ordinary residual sum / (ordinary opportunities + 240)
R = 100 × [0.75A + 0.25(A−B)]

Within each shared match-bootstrap draw, take the median of the nine scenario ratings. The final score is the 20th percentile of that distribution. The 90% band runs from its 5th to 95th percentile.

  • 60+ pressure opportunities

  • 120+ ordinary opportunities
  • 12+ matches overall
  • 2+ calendar years

Batting balls faced exclude wides and include no-balls. Bowling opportunities bundle preceding illegal deliveries with the next legal ball. Runs and wickets are measured from the bundle’s first pre-ball state. The full report documents exclusions and attribution.

A LIMIT THAT MATTERS

Historical pressure form
is not a permanent trait.

Strict earlier-versus-later rankings have weak persistence. The final lists summarize the supplied history, not a proven forecast of lifelong “clutch” ability.

Rank correlation uses players eligible in both periods. Resampling does not cover every source of model or selection uncertainty.

Frozen-selection backtests

Train on earlier seasons, freeze Top 10 selections, then measure future pressure residuals on later matches. Compare against pressure-only, overall-form and recent-form baselines.

Evaluation protocol

The results above replay 2024, 2025 and 2026 with expanding training windows. Run src/backtest.py locally.

02

Independent match coverage

A Cricsheet JSON adapter normalizes external T20 ball events into a separate local dataset. It does not silently merge player identities or claim validation before scoring parity is established.

Data source

Cricsheet match downloads. Official restricted competition data remains local and separate.

03

Bowling rotation scenarios

Use phase-level run estimates to optimize a legal 20-over bowling plan. This is an assumptions-driven scenario calculator, not an observed match result or an XI win predictor.

Simulation inputs

Provide 5-8 bowlers, estimated runs per over in each phase and an optional baseline. For external simulation context, see CricSynthesis documentation.
